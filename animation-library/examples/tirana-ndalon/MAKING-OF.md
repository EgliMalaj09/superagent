## The story (one idea)
Rain → Tirana stops → the numbers (1,000,000+ vehicles, 185 h/year in traffic, 200 L of fuel) → imagine the bus faster than
the car, 20 min home instead of 1 h 30 → "Nuk është mrekulli. Është planifikim." → the promised tram/train never built →
"Zgjidhjet i dimë. Mungon vetëm vendimi."

## Strongest points
1. **Match-cut opening** (the user's favourite): a real full-screen traffic map + rain → dive into one red line → cut to a row
   of brake lights in the 3D city. Beats a phone-on-dashboard POV and beats "screenshot tilting up into 3D".
2. **One continuous city** (isometric dusk): queued white cars, red traffic strips, a glowing **green bus lane** as the solution colour.
3. **Problem red → solution green** colour logic carried through every beat; promises crossed out (MARS 2024 / 2025).
4. Big condensed captions (Oswald caps) with one coloured highlight line — readable sound-off.
5. Voice leads: every move snapped to Whisper word times; bed ~15 dB under the voice, very few quiet hits.

## Rules learned here (now in TASTE.md)
- When the user brings their own opinion script: never fact-check it; wording suggestions for flow/hook are welcome.
- Script first, wait for approval; voice-over on top, quiet sound.
