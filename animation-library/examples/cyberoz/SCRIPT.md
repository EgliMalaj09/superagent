## Strongest points the video sells
1. **Live, continuous compliance** instead of an annual snapshot (the core idea).
2. **One platform** for assets, risks, controls, evidence and owners (vs spreadsheets and inboxes).
3. **Automation**: live integrations, automatic evidence, instant drift alerts.
4. **9 frameworks** incl. new EU rules (NIS2, DORA, AI Act).
5. **Trust**: built by security engineers, made in Frankfurt (Germany).

## Not used, on purpose
- No customer numbers, testimonials or "X% faster" claims (none on the site; we don't invent them).
