## Not used, on purpose
- "WHO 9 m²": no official WHO source.
- Rankings with Vienna 148 / Athens 45 m²: they count all vegetation, not parks; comparing them with Tirana would mislead.
- Population 1989 vs 2023: the borders changed in 2015, so the comparison isn't fair.
