# Style card: "car-decor" (blue gradient 3D motion, glossy navy objects, 9:16)

Source: a 33 s motivational reel the user liked (ref.mp4, sheet.png, strip.png). We take the style only.

## Look
- Background: soft periwinkle gradient (#F3F5FF top/white glow → #AFBDF5 → #7385EA), a deep-blue light pool in one corner,
  heavy depth-of-field blur on anything not in focus. Darker scenes: navy #0B1150 → near-black #04050F.
- Objects: glossy dark 3D objects with blue gradients and a thin bright rim (navy → electric blue #2E46E8): desk calendars,
  tilted cards, a liquid drip with a glass drop, a dark planet horizon with a bright blue rim, a silhouette with rim light,
  a tilted checklist. Line charts with a dot at the tip + faint grid + huge thin numbers.
- Palette: #F4F6FF · #AFBDF5 · #7385EA · #2E46E8 · #0B1150 · #04050F · white.

## Type
- Small caption top centre (Poppins Medium, ~44 px on 1080 w, navy or white) that carries the voice-over words.
- Bold labels on objects (Poppins Bold/ExtraBold, white on navy cards / navy on light).
- Huge thin numbers (Poppins ExtraLight, white at 60–80% opacity) for data.
- One hero word in tall condensed bold (Anton/Oswald 700 look), white, with a thin Poppins word overlapping it.
- Free fonts with ë/ç: Poppins (Google, latin-ext) · Oswald 700 (library).

## Motion
- Constant slow 3D camera drift; objects rotate in 3D (cards tilt, calendars flip/fall), depth-blur racks focus.
- Lines draw with a leading dot; numbers count; drips stretch and drop; text swaps word by word in the caption slot.
- Transitions: camera moves through/past objects, blur dissolves, the horizon rises to swap to dark scenes. No hard cuts.

## Don't copy
- Their exact sequence (discipline chart → calendars → "Same habits" cards → drip → "Everything Changes").
  Re-use the vocabulary for our own story.

## Conflicts with TASTE.md
- None serious: soft, no shakes, no flashes. Keep fast whip-blurs rare.
