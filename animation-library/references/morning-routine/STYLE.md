# Style card: "morning-routine" (B&W surreal photo-collage explainer, 9:16)

Source: a 35 s agency reel the user liked (ref.mp4, contact sheet sheet.png, strip.png). We take the style only.

## Look
- Background: soft off-white/grey paper (#ECEBE8 → #DAD8D3 at the edges), a faint square grid (≈90 px, ~5% black),
  a strong soft vignette, light film grain. Almost monochrome.
- Imagery: ONE black-and-white photo cut-out per beat, a surreal visual metaphor (paper boats, a thinking statue,
  a man with a CRT monitor for a head, a giant chess queen, a top-down meeting table, a handshake). Cut-outs sit on the
  paper with a soft contact shadow; some fade into mist/clouds at the bottom.
- The only colour: a small blue pushpin (#2E64D8) marking the key word. Everything else greyscale.
- Decoration in the reference: chrome 3D star shapes in the corners + the brand logo top centre → NOT copied.

## Type
- Geometric sans (Montserrat): a small Medium line above ("If you're", "But most") and a big Bold/ExtraBold word below
  ("Comparing", "Growth"). Weight + tone contrast: key word black #151515, second word mid-grey #6E6E6E.
- Sentence case (not caps), tight tracking, centred or left-aligned in the top third. Bullets use a small star glyph.
- Free match with ë/ç: **Montserrat** (Google Fonts, latin-ext) · fallback Outfit (library).

## Motion
- Text: focus-pull reveal (blur 14 px → 0 + slight scale 1.04 → 1, 0.4–0.5 s, power3.out); words leave by blurring out.
- Images: fade/blur in, slow push (scale 1 → 1.05 over the beat); one whip with motion blur as a transition (rare).
- New visual every ≈1.5–3 s, one idea per beat; lists build line by line.
- Transitions: soft blur-dissolve between beats; occasional fast whip-blur.

## Sound
- Light, minimal (the reference is music-led). For us: voice on top, quiet bed, no hits.

## Don't copy
- The logo, the chrome stars, their exact images (boats, statue, TV-head man, chess board) or layouts. Make our own
  metaphors for our story.

## Conflicts with TASTE.md
- Whip blurs: keep them rare and soft (no shakes, no flashes). Otherwise compatible (soft fades/blur, one quiet bed).
