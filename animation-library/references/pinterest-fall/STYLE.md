# Style card: "pinterest-fall" (monochrome chrome & glass 3D, dark → light, 9:16)

Source: an 11 s marketing reel the user liked (ref.mp4, sheet.png). We take the style only.

## Look
- Monochrome 3D: frosted-glass phone UIs stacked in depth, chrome cylinders/pedestals, stacks of silver coins, a desk
  calendar with a pencil, a wheel with ✕ marks and one green ✓, a light bulb that turns yellow, a crowd of silhouettes
  fading into white fog. Soft studio lighting, depth-of-field blur, light bloom.
- Background arc: starts near black (#050505) with huge concentric grey circles and bokeh orbs → mid grey → bright
  white fog at the end. The story literally goes from dark to light.
- Colour: none, except ONE accent at the payoff (yellow glow #F2C400, green ✓ #3BD16F).

## Type
- Big counting numbers in a bold geometric sans (Poppins SemiBold/Bold), grey-to-white gradient, with a small label under
  ("Views"). Very little text overall. Free match with ë/ç: Poppins.

## Motion
- Objects fly in and stack in 3D (glass phones), slow orbit/drift, numbers count up fast (142857 → 45 million+),
  coin stacks grow, the camera pulls back and racks focus; the scene brightens step by step; fog/blur dissolves.
- Pace: a new object every ~1–2 s; one continuous camera.

## Don't copy
- Their UI screens, the exact wheel/bulb/crowd sequence. Use the vocabulary for our own story.

## Conflicts with TASTE.md
- None: soft, no shakes, no flashes (the brightening is a slow ramp, not a flash).
