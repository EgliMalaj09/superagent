# Resources (free first)

## Fonts with Albanian ë / ç

Already in `library/fonts/`: Archivo Condensed, Bricolage Grotesque, DM Sans, Hanken Grotesk, Instrument Serif, Inter,
Oswald, Outfit, Source Serif 4, Special Elite, Spectral. Remotion: any Google Font via `@remotion/google-fonts` with
the `latin-ext` subset (Anton, Oswald, Unbounded, Space Grotesk, Archivo Black…). Always check one frame with "ËSHTË ÇFARË".

## Sound and music

- `library/sfx/` (synthesized, licence-free) + `reel-edit/scripts/sfx-synth.py` to make more; Remotion template's
  `scripts/generate-audio.mjs` synthesizes a full documentary score from a PLAN.
- Music beds: Pixabay Music, YouTube Audio Library (free); AI: Suno, ElevenLabs Music (check the plan's licence).
- SFX: Freesound (filter CC0), Pixabay sound effects, Mixkit.

## Stock footage / images

Pexels, Pixabay, Mixkit, Coverr (free licences; avoid clips labelled AI, readable third-party logos). Your own footage first.

## AI generation (the user runs these)

- Images with face likeness: Nano Banana Pro / Gemini image, Higgsfield (Soul, Banana Pro), Midjourney (`--cref`), GPT image, Flux.
- Video clips: Seedance, Kling, Veo, Runway. Prompt skills available in Claude: `kling-3-prompter`, `seedance-*`,
  `cinematic-film-prompt`, `json-prompter-video`, `nano-banana-prompter`, `raw-realism-prompter`, `banana-pro-director`.
- Voice: ElevenLabs (v3 + audio tags; see `docs/doc-reel/03-voiceover.md`).

## Research and data (Albania)

INSTAT (instat.gov.al), SPAK press releases, Balkan Insight / BIRN and Reporter.al, Faktoje.al (fact-checking),
Monitor.al (economy), Open Data Albania, European Western Balkans, Transparency International, wire services (Reuters, AP).
Always note the date of each figure and prefer the most recent published one.

## Inspiration

Behance motion graphics gallery, Dribbble animation tags, Motionographer, Pinterest video pins, the HyperFrames catalog
previews (`npx hyperframes catalog --json`). See `research/motion-design-2026.md` §4 for how to search and hand them over.
