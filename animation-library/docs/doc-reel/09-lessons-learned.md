# 09 — Lessons from building "Zoti i Shqipërisë"

What the user asked for, liked and rejected. Treat this as their taste profile.

## Collaboration

- Explanations in **English**, all video text and voice in **Albanian**. (They corrected this
  explicitly after a reply was written fully in Albanian.)
- They send short, fast messages and change direction (incinerators → recent scandals → neutral
  topics → back to a general Rama video). Re-pitch quickly; don't defend the old plan.
- They want to be told what to generate: exact prompts and exact filenames, then they paste images.
  Put the files in place yourself.
- They asked whether the music could be made by Claude. It can: synthesized in code. Be honest that
  it's a synth score, not orchestral.

## Story choices that landed

- A general "pattern" video beat a single case: one man, 13 years, minister after minister.
- The title concept "Zoti i Shqipërisë", chosen over literally calling him a dictator.
- 74 s of voice was fine. The user's instinct was "add scenes, not crop the audio".
- On-screen wording kept precise: "I DËNUAR" not "I BURGOSUR"; "SHISH përgjoi familjarët e
  prokurorëve" (as reported) rather than pinning it on a person.

## Visual feedback

- Loved: the 2013 rally hero shot, the parliament back-view, the portrait wall with stamps over the
  eyes, the split face, the money montage, the surveillance room.
- **Rejected:** the crown falling from the top onto his head ("the only part I don't like").
- **Rejected:** the replacement (gold god-rays + white flash cut to the crowned image).
- Current: a zoom-through (rush into his face, cut, pull back to reveal the crown). Alternative
  ready: a face-matched dissolve (see 05). Ask which one they want before redoing it.
- Crown text: first "Mbreti bythqir" (a crude joke they wanted emphasized), then a new crown reading
  "MBRETI I SHQIPËRISË". Always confirm the exact engraved text.
- They removed the crown-text close-up scene and the "SHPËRNDAJE. MOS HESHT." outro. The video now
  ends on the crowned throne + "ZOTI I SHQIPËRISË" with a fade.

## Final cleanup (2026-10-08)

- On request, every screen shake and red/white flash was removed from the Zoti reel: the split-face shake, the
  red flash on each stamp, the white→red cut flashes in the money montage (now 10-frame crossfades) and the
  shake + red flash after the crown reveal. This matches TASTE.md (no shakes, no flashes).

## Sound feedback

- SFX were "really interfering" with the voice, so the master SFX gain was cut to 0.3. Keep effects subtle.

## Process lessons

- Measure the voiceover first (pause detection). It made every cut land on the word.
- Render stills and look at them before reporting. That caught the checkerboard inside the crown
  arches (fixed by the enclosed-pocket keying) and the burned-in filename on the tunnel sign.
- Generated "before/after" pairs don't share framing unless you edit the first image. Plan the
  transition around that.
