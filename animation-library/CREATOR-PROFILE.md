# Creator profile: who I am, what I make, what I like

> Drop this file into any Claude Code project (or paste it into CLAUDE.md) so the assistant knows how I work.
> Distilled from real projects made in October 2026: Tirana traffic, Tirana housing, the Zoti i Shqipërisë exposé,
> Zvërnec, and two epara app ads. Newest lessons win when two rules disagree.

---

## 1. Who I am and what I make

- **Creator of short-form video** (Instagram Reels first, also LinkedIn) for an **Albanian audience**.
- **Main genres**
  - **Opinion / social-issue explainers about Albania and Tirana:** traffic, housing prices, urban planning, public promises.
    Built on my own scripts and my own voice-over.
  - **Political exposés / documentaries:** voice-led, cinematic, AI-generated stills ("Zoti i Shqipërisë").
  - **Product / app ads:** short (10–12 s) promos for mobile apps (e.g. epara, a BKT digital wallet).
  - Occasional character skits and talking-head reel edits.
- **Tools:** Claude Code does the building. HyperFrames (HTML + GSAP → MP4) for motion graphics, Remotion for AI-image
  documentaries, FFmpeg + Whisper for audio/sync. **Free, local tools only.**
- **I generate images and record voice myself** (Nano Banana Pro / other image models, my phone mic). Give me exact
  prompts and exact filenames; I paste the files back and you put them into the project.

## 2. How to work with me (process)

1. **Talk to me in English.** Everything spoken or written on screen is in the video's language (usually **Albanian**;
   product ads usually **English**).
2. **Script first.** Before any animation, show the script / storyboard scene by scene (time · words · what we see) and
   wait for my OK. For a new look, show **2–3 style frames (stills)** before building the whole video.
3. **Explain each animation sentence by sentence** when I ask; I decide from that.
4. **My own script = my opinion.** Never fact-check or question the numbers in a script I bring. Wording suggestions for
   flow and hook are welcome.
5. **Your research = sourced.** When you write the numbers (data videos), use only real, sourced figures, show the
   source small on screen, say the year, and list "not used, on purpose" numbers that would mislead. Never invent
   claims, testimonials or statistics. Product ads: only claims that appear in the brand's own material.
6. **Be critical when I ask.** "Have a critical thinking" means: rank the real weaknesses (emotion, scale, monotony,
   hook, collisions), propose fixes, then let me choose which to do.
7. **Verify by looking.** Render still frames / a contact sheet before every full render and fix what you see (text
   collisions, cut-off labels, objects hiding the subject). Say honestly what you did and did not check.
8. **Keep versions.** Before a big change, keep the previous version (variants/) so we can go back.
9. **Sync to my voice at the end.** Build with placeholder timing; when the recording arrives, transcribe (Whisper) and
   move every beat onto its word. If my recording is longer, **add visual beats, never slow animations down or cut audio**.
10. **Save taste immediately.** When I like or dislike something, write it down the same turn and tell me in one line.
11. **Keep replies short; spend tokens on the work, not on narration.**
12. **Finished project:** keep only the final MP4 + cover, the sources, and a MAKING-OF.md. Move intermediates to the
    Trash (never `rm`).

## 3. What I like (visual taste)

- **Isometric 3D worlds** (a small dusk/night city, queued cars, lit windows, cut-away apartments): my favourite.
  **But not for a whole video alone.** Mix by purpose:
  - **isometric** = story, world, emotion (the couple, the apartment, the city)
  - **big kinetic type / number cards** = one number at a time
  - **simple, honest charts** (bars to scale) = comparisons
  One palette and one font across all three so it reads as one film.
- **Premium / "luxurious" finish** (what made the housing reel jump in quality):
  - soft contact shadows, warm light from inside, haze in the distance, depth-of-field blur at the frame edges
  - champagne gold instead of flat yellow; deep night navy; subtle colour grade (cool shadows, warm highlights);
    light moving film grain + soft vignette
  - **60 fps**, slower eases (0.8–1.2 s), no bounce/overshoot on labels, the camera never fully stops, a little motion
    blur on camera dives
  - Oswald (numbers, headlines) + **Instrument Serif italic** for the human lines; wide letter-spacing on small labels
  - frosted-glass cards over the blurred world instead of flat full-screen cards
  - a living world: street lamps, small cars with headlights, trees, windows switching on/off
- **Visual metaphors that make numbers felt:** a month's salary = a doormat on an 80 m² floor; a year = a bathroom;
  27 years = a timeline where the 8-year mark shrinks; the couple's hair turning grey.
- **Strong, specific hooks:** open with the viewer's own question word for word, and show the payoff immediately (e.g. a
  whole life in fast-forward in 3 s, then rewind and tell the story). Match cuts beat literal POVs.
- **Vox-style paper explainer** (paper, ink, yellow highlighter, white-bordered photos) for documentary/political pieces.
- **Dark news style** (black + red, Oswald caps) for investigative pieces.
- **AI-image cinematic documentary:** slow pushes, film grain, a portrait wall with stamps, a strong title concept used
  as cold open and final line.
- **Spider-Verse comic look** for character skits.
- **One continuous camera move** through a world beats cutting between slides.
- **Product ads:** the brand's real assets (logo SVG, card design, app screenshots, style-guide colours, app font),
  real store availability, demo amounts clearly treated as demo. Focus one ad on **one selling point** and show it
  through repetition (e.g. many different ATMs → same "Fee 0,00 ALL" every time).

## 4. What I don't like

- **Screen shakes. Red or white flashes.** Use soft fades/slides (0.3–0.45 s, power3.out) and 0.35–0.4 s crossfades.
- **Sound effects on text or slide entrances**, stamp/boom hits on every red word, loud SFX over the voice, ambience
  beds (café/crowd). Voice on top, one quiet music bed (~0.07 when the voice is 1.0, about 15 dB under), SFX ≤ 0.1 and only on real moments.
- **A list of numbers in a row.** One number per beat, each turned into a picture; extra numbers small on screen.
- **Weak, slow or abstract openings** (a wide shot of nothing, a key spinning in the void, a flat screenshot tilting
  into 3D, a phone-on-dashboard POV).
- **Text that collides** with other labels or gets cut off at the frame edge; objects/buildings hiding the subject.
- **Everything in one style for the whole video** (too much isometric, too much chrome).
- Styles tried and rejected for the housing reel: chrome & glass monochrome for the whole video, 3-layer photo parallax
  opening.
- Outros that repeat the punchline after the title has landed; gimmicky transitions (falling crowns, god-ray flashes).
- Videos that drag: when in doubt, about 10% faster.

## 5. Formats and delivery

- **Instagram Reels: 9:16, 1080×1920**, keep text inside the safe zone (top ~220 px, bottom ~380 px free).
- **LinkedIn: 4:5, 1080×1350.**
- Length: opinion/data reels 50–70 s are fine if every beat earns its place; product ads 10–12 s.
- Final export: −14 LUFS, BT.709, CBR ~12 Mb/s, faststart, plus a designed cover frame (pick the most emotional frame,
  e.g. the payoff).
- Finished videos go to `results/`; each video has its own `projects/<name>/` folder.

## 6. Albanian specifics

- Diacritics ë/ç must render (choose fonts that support them: Oswald, Poppins, Plus Jakarta Sans, Instrument Serif,
  Montserrat, Outfit…).
- Number format: `2.141 €`, `171.000 €`, `0,41 m²`, `1.070,90 ALL`.
- Whisper mis-hears Albanian (e.g. "Tiranandalon", "qifti"); use word timings for sync, not the text.
- Never target a people, religion or private person; criticise decisions and public figures with evidence; satire is
  fine, fabricated events are not.

## 7. Technical best practices learned (HyperFrames / GSAP)

- Snapshot/contact sheet before every render; check every scene for collisions and occlusion.
- Billboard labels in a rotating isometric world must counter-rotate with the world (rotation = −spin, rotationX = −tilt).
- Keep buildings low in front of the subject so the camera never loses it.
- Don't reuse engine helper names (`tree`, `label`…) in scene code: a duplicate `const` silently kills the whole page.
- Gradients can't be tweened: fade a separate layer's opacity instead.
- `mix-blend-mode: screen` inside a CSS-3D world flattens the 3D: avoid it there.
- Kit classes can carry hidden styles (`.photo` = greyscale, `.chip`/`.bar` sizes): use unique class names.
- Retime a finished timeline to a voice with a piecewise `remap([[old,new],…])` built from Whisper word times.
- Logos built for light backgrounds disappear on dark ones: put them on a light tile.
- When a site renders empty (client-side app + consent banner), decline cookies and use the App Store / Play listing as
  the source of truth.
- Render at 60 fps with `--fps 60` (and set `data-fps="60"`).

## 8. Workflow files I keep in every project

- `SCRIPT.md`: storyboard + sources + "not used, on purpose".
- `VO-FINAL.md`: the text I record + line-by-line tone notes (calm, slightly ironic, heavier with each worse number,
  pauses before the punch lines, end in silence).
- `STORYBOARD-*.md`: sentence-by-sentence animation plan for each candidate style.
- `references/<name>/STYLE.md`: style cards extracted from reference videos I send.
- `variants/`: earlier versions kept for comparison.
- `MAKING-OF.md`: written at the end.
