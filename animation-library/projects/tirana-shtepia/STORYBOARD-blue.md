# Sa vjet për një shtëpi në Tiranë? — animation storyboard (blue 3D style, ≈60 s, 9:16)

Style: references/car-decor/STYLE.md. One continuous world: a soft blue gradient space with a white floor; the camera
drifts the whole time (no hard cuts). Captions small at the top (Poppins) say the voice-over words; objects are glossy
navy with a bright blue rim; data in huge thin numbers; one tall condensed hero word for the big moments.
Motion: soft (0.4–0.6 s, power3.out), focus pulls / depth blur, no shakes, no flashes, no sound effects on text.
Sound: your voice + one quiet bed.

## 1 · Hook (≈0–4 s)
**"Sa vjet i duhen një çifti në Tiranë për të blerë një apartament?"**
- Caption appears word by word with a soft focus pull.
- A glossy navy 3D house floats in the middle of the blue space, turning slowly; two small glossy spheres (the couple) at
  its base. Behind it, a huge thin **?** sits out of focus.
- On "apartament?" the camera eases toward the house and the hero text lands: thin "Sa" over tall bold **VJET?**

## 2 · Price per m² (≈4–10 s)
**"Një metër katror në Tiranë kushton sot mesatarisht mbi 2 mijë euro."**
- The camera drifts down to the white floor. A glossy navy 1×1 m slab drops in and settles softly; thin dimension
  lines draw: "1 m" × "1 m".
- A huge thin number counts **0 → 2.141 €** under it.
**"Më shumë se kurrë më parë."**
- Behind the slab a line chart draws from left to right with a glowing dot on its tip, ending at the highest point;
  a small glossy card tilts in: **REKORD**.

## 3 · Salary → doormat (≈10–17 s)
**"Paga mesatare? Rreth 880 euro në muaj."**
- A glossy card slides in tilted in 3D: "Paga mesatare" → it flips over: **880 € / muaj**.
**"Pra, një muaj rrogë blen sa një tapet dere."**
- A beam of light scans across the 1 m² slab; only a small corner stays bright (0,41 m²), the rest turns to see-through glass.
- A dark doormat ("MIRË SE VINI" pressed into it) slides onto the bright corner. Thin **0,41 m²**.

## 4 · One year → bathroom (≈17–21 s)
**"Një vit punë? Një banjo."**
- Twelve small glossy tiles labelled JAN … DHJ fall one after another in rhythm and click into a grid.
- The grid lifts into a minimal glossy bathroom (tub + sink shapes). Thin **4,9 m²**.

## 5 · The apartment, 8 years (≈21–30 s)
**"Një apartament 2+1, 80 metra katrorë, kushton rreth 171 mijë euro."**
- The camera rises and tilts down: an 80 m² floor plan with glass walls stands on the white floor (2 rooms, living room,
  kitchen); the bathroom from scene 4 slides into its place.
- A price card swings in on a thin line: **171.000 €**.
**"Edhe sikur të mos shpenzonin asnjë lek, një çift me dy rroga mesatare do t'i mblidhte për 8 vjet."**
- A desk calendar flips fast **2026 → 2034**; with each flip a wave of glossy blue tiles fills the plan.
- A grey glossy card: **pa shpenzuar asnjë lek**. Thin **8 vjet**.

## 6 · Real life: 27 years (≈30–39 s)
**"Por jeta kushton."**
- The tiles sink back through the floor, the light dims a little.
**"Nëse kursejnë 30% të pagës, duhen 27 vjet."**
- Card: **kursejnë 30%**. Tiles start refilling, much slower.
- Desk calendars fall one after another in 3D: **2026 → 2040 → 2053** (blurred → sharp). Huge thin **27 vjet**.
**"Nisin në 25. Çelësin e marrin në 52."**
- Two glossy chips connected by a glowing line: **25 ———→ 52**.
- On "52" the last tile lands and a glossy key drops onto the finished floor.

## 7 · Minimum wage (≈39–46 s)
**"Dhe kjo me pagë mesatare. Një në katër të punësuar marrin pagën minimale."**
- Four glossy capsule figures stand in a row; one of them lights up electric blue: **1 në 4**.
**"Për ta, shtëpia kërkon mbi 50 vjet punë. Një jetë të tërë."**
- The dark planet rises from the bottom and fills the lower half; a blue drip stretches down from the top; on "mbi 50 vjet"
  a glass drop falls and touches the planet's rim.
- Hero: thin "Një jetë" over tall bold **TË TËRË.**

## 8 · Prices since 2019 (≈46–53 s)
**"Ndërkohë, çmimet nuk presin. Në 2019, i njëjti apartament kushtonte 77 mijë euro. Sot, 171 mijë."**
- The camera rises out of the dark back into the blue. A line chart draws with a glowing dot:
  **2019 · 77.000 €** → **2026 · 171.000 €**.
**"Më shumë se dyfish."**
- Huge thin **+122%**; a ghost copy of the 2019 point doubles upward to show "more than double".

## 9 · Close (≈53–60 s)
**"Një brez i tërë po punon për një çelës."**
- Many small glossy capsule figures (a whole generation) flow slowly toward one glowing key floating in the middle.
**"Kur do të jetë e jona?"**
- The camera pushes slowly toward the key; hero: thin "Kur do të jetë" over tall bold **E JONA?**
- Hold in silence ~1 s; small source line at the bottom:
  "Keydata T1 2026 · INSTAT T2 2026 · pa interes kredie, pa inflacion".
