# Sa vjet për një shtëpi në Tiranë? — storyboard in the chrome & glass style (references/pinterest-fall), ≈60 s

## The rules of this style
- Monochrome 3D: frosted-glass phones/cards, chrome pedestals, silver coin stacks, a desk calendar. Soft studio light,
  depth blur, slow bloom. One continuous camera that drifts and pulls back; no hard cuts.
- The background tells the story: **black (scenes 1–4) → dark grey (5) → mid grey (6–8) → white fog (9)**, a slow ramp,
  never a flash.
- Text: big counting numbers in Poppins Bold with a chrome gradient + one small label under them; a small caption line
  at the top carries the voice-over.
- No colour until the very end: the key turns **yellow** in the last scene (the only colour in the film).
- Soft motion (0.4–0.6 s, power3.out), no shakes, no sound effects on text; your voice + one quiet bed.

## Sentence by sentence

### 1 · Hook — black
**"Sa vjet i duhen një çifti në Tiranë për të blerë një apartament?"**
- Black screen; huge grey concentric rings slowly open from the centre, a few bokeh lights float.
- A frosted-glass phone flies in from deep in the dark and turns to face us: a property app, search "Tiranë · 2+1".
- The big number above it rolls like a slot machine (8 … 19 … 27 … 52 …) and stops on **? vjet**.

### 2 · Price per m² — black
**"Një metër katror në Tiranë kushton sot mesatarisht mbi 2 mijë euro."**
- Two more glass phones slide in behind the first and stack in depth (listings).
- The big number counts fast **0 → 2.141 €**, label under it: **për m² · Tiranë**.
**"Më shumë se kurrë më parë."**
- Below, chrome columns rise one after another (2019 … 2026); the last one is the tallest and a small glass tag lands
  on it: **REKORD**.

### 3 · Salary → doormat — black
**"Paga mesatare? Rreth 880 euro në muaj."**
- The phones blur away; a short stack of silver coins drops onto a glass floor tile. Number: **880 €**, label **paga / muaj**.
**"Pra, një muaj rrogë blen sa një tapet dere."**
- A glass square "1 m²" lights up on the floor; the coins can only buy a small corner of it. A chrome doormat rises in
  that corner. Number: **0,41 m²**, label **sa një tapet dere**.

### 4 · One year → bathroom — black
**"Një vit punë? Një banjo."**
- Twelve small coin stacks drop in a row, one per month, in rhythm.
- The camera pulls back: they melt into a small chrome bathroom (tub + sink) on a pedestal. Number: **4,9 m²**.

### 5 · The apartment, 8 years — black → dark grey
**"Një apartament 2+1, 80 metra katrorë, kushton rreth 171 mijë euro."**
- The light rises a little. A glass floor plan of the apartment floats over a chrome base; the bathroom slides into
  its place. Number counts **171.000 €**, label **2+1 · 80 m²**.
**"Edhe sikur të mos shpenzonin asnjë lek, … do t'i mblidhte për 8 vjet."**
- A desk calendar on a chrome cylinder flips fast **2026 → 2034** while coin stacks grow around it.
  Number: **8 vjet**, label **pa shpenzuar asnjë lek**.

### 6 · Real life: 27 years — mid grey
**"Por jeta kushton."**
- The coin stacks sink to a third of their height; the light softens.
**"Nëse kursejnë 30% të pagës, duhen 27 vjet."**
- Label **kursejnë 30%**. The calendar flips slowly, a pencil crossing off days; the coins grow slowly again.
  Number counts **27 vjet**.
**"Nisin në 25. Çelësin e marrin në 52."**
- The number changes to an age counter **25 → 52**; on "52" the calendar stops on **2053** and a chrome key lands on
  the last coin stack.

### 7 · Minimum wage — mid grey
**"Dhe kjo me pagë mesatare. Një në katër të punësuar marrin pagën minimale."**
- A chrome wheel with four segments turns in; three segments stay silver, one lights up darker: label **1 në 4 · paga minimale**.
**"Për ta, shtëpia kërkon mbi 50 vjet punë. Një jetë të tërë."**
- The number jumps past 27 to **50+ vjet**; the camera slowly pulls back and the wheel looks small.
  Label: **një jetë të tërë**.

### 8 · Prices since 2019 — mid grey
**"Ndërkohë, çmimet nuk presin. Në 2019, i njëjti apartament kushtonte 77 mijë euro. Sot, 171 mijë."**
- Two glass listing cards side by side: **2019 · 77.000 €** (smaller, dimmer) and **2026 · 171.000 €** (bigger, brighter).
**"Më shumë se dyfish."**
- The 2026 card grows to more than twice the height. Number: **+122%**.

### 9 · Close — white fog
**"Një brez i tërë po punon për një çelës."**
- The whole scene brightens slowly to white fog; a crowd of grey silhouettes rises out of the fog at the bottom
  (a generation), looking up.
**"Kur do të jetë e jona?"**
- Above them a key floats; it slowly turns **yellow** and glows (the only colour in the film).
  Big text **Kur do të jetë e jona?** Hold ~1 s, small sources at the bottom:
  "Keydata T1 2026 · INSTAT T2 2026 · pa interes kredie, pa inflacion".
