# Sa vjet për një shtëpi në Tiranë? — Vox paper storyboard v1 (≈60 s, 9:16)

## The look
- One big sheet of cream paper (#EEE8DC, fine grain + light halftone). The whole film is ONE continuous camera gliding over
  this sheet (like a desk collage); every scene is a new spot on the same paper. At the end we pull back and see it all.
- Black ink line drawings that draw themselves; yellow highlighter (#FFD23F) swipes on the key words; red ink (#C8202F) only
  for bad news (circles, crossings).
- Photos are black-and-white prints with a white border, slightly tilted, with a strip of masking tape.
- Type: Archivo Condensed 800 caps for headlines, Source Serif italic for the small human lines, Hanken for sources.
- Motion: soft (0.35–0.45 s, power3.out), glides 0.8 s between scenes. No shakes, no flashes, no sound effects on text;
  one quiet bed under the voice.

## Frame by frame

### 1 · Hook (0–4 s) — "Sa vjet i duhen një çifti në Tiranë për të blerë një apartament?"
1. 0.0 — Close on paper. An ink outline of a simple house (roof + square) draws itself in the middle (1 s).
2. 0.3 — Headline writes in word by word above it: **SA VJET I DUHEN / NJË ÇIFTI NË TIRANË**.
3. 1.6 — A B&W print of a young couple (25) slides in from the right, tilted −4°; a tape strip lands on its corner.
4. 2.4 — **PËR NJË APARTAMENT?** appears; the yellow highlighter swipes under it left → right.
5. 3.2 — A big red-ink **?** draws itself inside the house outline.
6. 3.8 — The camera glides down the paper to the next spot.

### 2 · Price per m² (4–10 s) — "…mbi 2 mijë euro. Më shumë se kurrë më parë."
1. An ink square draws itself with architect's dimension arrows: **1 m × 1 m**.
2. The highlighter fills the square yellow in one stroke.
3. **2.141 €** writes in beside it in heavy type; tiny source under it: *Keydata, T1 2026*.
4. On "më shumë se kurrë": a small hand-drawn line (2019 → 2026) climbs; a red pen circles its top: **REKORD**.

### 3 · Salary → doormat (10–17 s) — "Paga mesatare? Rreth 880 euro… sa një tapet dere."
1. A paper payslip (white card with ruled lines) slides in: **PAGA MESATARE NETO ≈ 880 €**, highlighter on 880.
2. An ink arrow draws from the payslip to the yellow square.
3. The yellow fill shrinks back until only a small square is left (0.41 m²); the rest of the square stays dashed and empty.
4. A cut-out photo of a doormat drops onto the small square with a soft paper shadow; serif italic note: *sa një tapet dere*.

### 4 · One year → bathroom (17–21 s) — "Një vit punë? Një banjo."
1. Twelve small paper tiles labelled JAN … DHJ fly in one after another and snap into a grid.
2. The grid flips over and becomes a small B&W print of a tiny bathroom; **1 VIT = 4,9 m²** with highlighter.

### 5 · The apartment, 8 years (21–30 s) — "Një apartament 2+1, 80 metra katrorë… 8 vjet."
1. The camera pulls back: an ink floor plan of a 2+1 draws itself (walls, door arcs, room names: DHOMË · DHOMË · SALLON ·
   KUZHINË · BANJO); the bathroom print from scene 4 sits exactly in the BANJO room.
2. A paper price tag on a string swings in from the top: **171.000 €**.
3. A tear-off calendar counts 0 → 8 years; with every year the highlighter fills the plan, row by row.
4. A small grey sticky note lands on the corner: *pa shpenzuar asnjë lek*.

### 6 · Real life: 27 years, 25 → 52 (30–39 s) — "Por jeta kushton… Çelësin e marrin në 52."
1. "Por jeta kushton": a red pen crosses out the sticky note; an eraser wipes the yellow fill away.
2. A handwritten note appears: **kursejnë 30%**.
3. The fill restarts, much slower; big ink numerals count **0 → 27 VJET**.
4. Under the plan, the young couple print (from scene 1) is joined by a second print: the same couple at 52, grey hair.
   An ink timeline draws between them: **25 ———→ 52**.
5. On "52": the last highlighter stroke completes the plan and a cut-out key drops onto it.

### 7 · Minimum wage (39–46 s) — "Një në katër… mbi 50 vjet punë. Një jetë të tërë."
1. Four little ink figures draw in a row; the red pen circles one: **1 NË 4 · PAGË MINIMALE**.
2. The counter keeps running: 27 → **50+**; the timeline line under the photos keeps extending until it runs off the paper.
3. Serif italic, alone: *Një jetë të tërë.*

### 8 · Prices since 2019 (46–53 s) — "Ndërkohë, çmimet nuk presin… Më shumë se dyfish."
1. Two receipts get pinned to the paper: **2019 · 77.000 €** (yellowed, faded) and **2026 · 171.000 €** (fresh, white).
2. The 2026 receipt stretches upward like a bar until it is more than twice as tall; red ink arrow: **+122%**.
3. Highlighter on **MË SHUMË SE DYFISH**.

### 9 · Close (53–60 s) — "Një brez i tërë po punon për një çelës. Kur do të jetë e jona?"
1. The camera pulls back slowly and reveals the whole sheet: every piece of the story is one collage (house, square,
   payslip, doormat, bathroom, plan, the two couple photos, receipts).
2. In the middle, the headline **KUR DO TË JETË E JONA?** with a yellow highlighter swipe; the key print under it.
3. Small source line at the bottom: *Keydata T1 2026 · INSTAT T2 2026 · pa interes kredie, pa inflacion.*
