channels, and I want that work to turn into paid work for brands. I work on a Mac (M2 Pro) and finish edits in CapCut.

What I've learned from my own numbers:
- **Albanian culture made with AI travels** (30K–845K views). Generic AI clips with no story stall at 1–4K.
  The audience rewards AI that says something about them, teaches them, or shows something they didn't think possible.
- My typical reel: 1.5K–4.5K views. My past Claude animation with no story: 3,271. An animation needs a job.

Rules for every video:
- One idea per video, a reason to care in the first 2 seconds, one ask (save, share, or comment a keyword).
- Albanian first. Explainers are in Albanian; pure-AI pieces can be English with Albanian subtitles.
- Original work only, never reposts.
- No real politicians in realistic AI footage. Turn on Instagram's AI label when footage looks real.
- Only say numbers and facts I've checked.

## How I like to work
- Talk to me in English, short. On-screen and spoken content is usually Albanian.
- Script / storyboard first; I approve before anything is built or paid for.
- Smooth motion, voice first, no screen shakes, no white or red flashes.
- I batch: face videos in one filming session, AI pieces two days before they post.
- Suggest the animation style yourself, with the reason; I decide from your suggestion.
- When a video is finished, keep only the script, why that style was chosen, and the final result. Clean the rest
  automatically (to the Trash).
