The Ottoman envoy rides in through the open courtyard gate on a white horse, followed by three Ottoman horsemen in ultramarine and bone-white with tall caps and lances. The envoy holds up a rolled firman sealed with red wax. In the foreground, seen from behind and to one side, Gjon Kastrioti stands waiting in his black coat and red cloak, one hand on his dagger. Low angle, hard sun, long shadows across the earth courtyard.
