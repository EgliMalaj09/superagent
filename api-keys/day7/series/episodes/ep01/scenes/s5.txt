Young Gjergj rides out of the courtyard on a dark horse, surrounded by Ottoman horsemen dressed exactly like the envoy's men: tall bone-white Ottoman caps, ultramarine and bone-white kaftans, lances, no European armour, no red cloaks on any of them. The boy is the ONLY figure wearing red: his long vermilion cloak spills over the horse's flank. He twists in the saddle to look back over his shoulder toward camera. Behind him, small in the open gateway under the stone tower, his father Gjon stands alone and motionless in his plain black coat WITHOUT any cloak (he has just given it to his son). Hard sun, dust on the ground. No flags or banners.
