HOUSE OF ALBANIA · S1 Ep 1/10 — The Hostage (c.1423)

In the mountains of Arbëria, Gjon Kastrioti ruled his lands under the shadow of the Ottoman Empire. To keep them, Sultan Murad II demanded the one tribute a father cannot refuse: his sons.

One of them was Gjergj.

He was taken to the Sultan's court, given a new faith and a new name, Iskender, and raised to fight for the empire that took him.

He would not see these mountains again for twenty years. And when he came back, nothing would be the same.
