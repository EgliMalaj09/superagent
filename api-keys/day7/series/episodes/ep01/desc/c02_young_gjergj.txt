Young Gjergj Kastrioti, Albanian noble boy of about 16, lean and tall for his age, dark short hair, intense dark eyes, a serious still face, the first trace of the same strong nose and brow as the adult Skanderbeg. Wears a 15th-century Albanian highland noble's outfit: long bone-white wool tunic to the knee with dark embroidered trim, black wool waistcoat, wide red sash, dark wool trousers and leather opinga shoes. Over his shoulders a long vermilion red wool cloak that is clearly too big for him, its hem dragging on the ground.
