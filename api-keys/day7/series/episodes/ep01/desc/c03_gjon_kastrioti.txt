Gjon Kastrioti, 15th-century Albanian highland lord, about 55, broad and weathered, heavy dark moustache and short grey-shot beard, deep-set tired eyes, grave and proud. Wears a long dark black wool coat with silver clasps and dark embroidered trim over a bone-white linen shirt, wide leather belt with a dagger, dark trousers, leather boots, and a long vermilion red wool cloak fastened at the shoulder with a round silver brooch.
