## Format rules (every episode)
- **Image posts:** Instagram carousel, **3:4**, 6–8 slides per episode. Slide 1 = cover, last slide = hook. Images carry no text; title and captions are added on top in editing.
- **Video (when an episode needs it):** generated with **Google Omni** (user's API), **9:16**.
- No dialogue, and no text generated inside the images or video.
- Every episode ends on a held "poster frame" (works as the IG cover) and a hook into the next one.
- Recurring motif: **the red cloak**. Gjon gives it to Gjergj in Ep 1; it is in every episode; it is laid over him in Ep 10.
- Recurring motif: **the eagle** — seen overhead at every turning point.
- Caption template: `HOUSE OF ALBANIA · S1 Ep N/10 — <Title> (<year>)` + 2–3 lines of real history + "Follow for Ep N+1".

## Historical baseline (verify before posting; legend marked as such)
- c.1405 born to Gjon Kastrioti, lord in the Mat/Dibër region.
- c.1423 sent with brothers as hostage to the court of Sultan Murad II. Tradition (Barleti) says as a child; modern historians say ~18. **Series choice: a teenager (~16) — keeps it emotional and closer to the scholarship.**
- Converted, trained, named **Iskender Bey**; 1440 sanjakbey of Dibra.
- Nov 1443 Battle of Niš: deserts with ~300 Albanian horsemen, forces a scribe to forge a firman, takes Krujë on **28 Nov 1443**, raises the red flag with the black double-headed eagle (the flag part is tradition).
- **2 Mar 1444** League of Lezhë — chief commander.
- **29 Jun 1444** Battle of Torvioll — first victory (~15,000 vs ~40,000 per traditional numbers).
- 1450 First Siege of Krujë — Murad II fails; commander of the garrison Vrana Konti.
- Apr 1451 marries **Donika Arianiti**; Treaty of Gaeta with Naples.
- 1456 nephew **Hamza Kastrioti** defects to the Ottomans; **2 Sep 1457** Battle of Ujëbardha (Albulena) — great victory, Hamza captured and spared.
- 1460–61 campaign in Italy for Ferdinand of Naples. 1463 named commander of Pius II's crusade (pope dies, crusade collapses).
- 1466–67 Mehmed II besieges Krujë twice; fails.
- **17 Jan 1468** dies of fever in Lezhë, buried in St Nicholas church. Krujë falls 1478. Legend: Ottoman soldiers later took his bones as amulets.

## The 10 core episodes

**Ep 1 — The Hostage (c.1423)**
Gjon Kastrioti hands his son to the Sultan's envoy. Before the boy rides away, the father puts his own red cloak on him. Last beat: the boy looks back, and his face match-cuts to the adult Skanderbeg in the goat helmet.
Hook: *"They took a boy. They'll get back a storm."*

**Ep 2 — Iskender (1423–1440)**
Montage at the Ottoman court: the boy is renamed, trained, and becomes the Sultan's finest commander, Iskender Bey. He wins every fight but keeps the red cloak hidden in his chest.
Hook: the Sultan's favour, and a look toward the west.

**Ep 3 — Niš (Nov 1443)**
The Ottoman line breaks at Niš. In the chaos Iskender rides into the scribe's tent and forces him to seal a false firman. He turns his 300 Albanian riders west into the snow.
Hook: the forged seal pressed into red wax.

**Ep 4 — The Flag over Krujë (28 Nov 1443)**
The firman opens the gates of Krujë. At dawn the Sultan's banner comes down and the red flag with the black eagle goes up. He says (via caption, not dialogue): *"I have not brought you freedom — I found it here, among you."* (Barleti tradition.)
Hook: the crowd kneels; he makes them stand.

**Ep 5 — The League of Lezhë (2 Mar 1444)**
Rival Albanian lords who hate each other gather in a church in Lezhë. One by one, they lay their swords at his feet.
Hook: a rider arrives with news of an Ottoman army of 40,000.

**Ep 6 — Torvioll (29 Jun 1444)**
A feigned retreat draws the Ottomans into the valley, and hidden riders pour out of the forest. It's the first victory.
Hook: the Sultan in Edirne crushes the report in his fist.

**Ep 7 — The Siege (1450)**
Sultan Murad II surrounds Krujë with a vast army. Vrana Konti holds the walls while Skanderbeg strikes the camps from the mountains at night. The Sultan retreats and dies the next year.
Hook: the Sultan leaves; his son Mehmed watches from afar.

**Ep 8 — Donika (1451)**
A quiet episode: his wedding to Donika Arianiti, with the alliance sealed and the red cloak around both of them. One tender beat before the storm.
Hook: a letter arrives with Hamza's seal.

**Ep 9 — The Betrayal (1456–57)**
His nephew Hamza defects and marches with the Ottomans against him. Skanderbeg lets them think he's beaten, then strikes at Ujëbardha. Hamza is captured, and Skanderbeg spares him.
Hook: forgiveness, shown in one gesture.

**Ep 10 — The Last Lion (17 Jan 1468)**
In Lezhë, fevered, he gives his helmet and sword to his young son. He dies. The red cloak is laid over him. Krujë is shown standing, though it is doomed to fall in 1478. The eagle rises over the mountains.
Hook: *"The story didn't end with him."* This leaves the door open for season 2.

- The Italian campaign (1460–61): Skanderbeg in Apulia.
- Moisi Golemi's betrayal at Berat (1455–56).
- The Siege of Berat defeat (1455): a dark episode.
- Mehmed II's sieges 1466–67 & the founding of Elbasan.
- After Skanderbeg: the fall of Krujë (1478), Lekë Dukagjini, the Arbëreshë exodus to Italy.
- Donika and their son Gjon Kastrioti II in exile in Naples.

1. Write `episodes/epNN/plan.md` (beats, needed characters/locations, which exist in the registry).
2. Make each new asset: `python3 make_asset.py character|location desc.txt out.png` from `day7/`.
3. Review and lock the assets, then update the registries above.
4. Generate the carousel slides at 3:4 (`episodes/epNN/slides/`), with the character sheets attached as references.
5. If the episode becomes a video: write `episodes/epNN/omni_prompt.md` (9:16) and keep the take where the texture survives the motion.
