## CHARACTER SHEET BLOCK

3-panel costume reference sheet of a real photographed actor on a flat saturated ultramarine backdrop with print grain, like the sky in the references: panel 1 front-facing head-and-shoulders close-up, panels 2 and 3 full-body front view and back view, same character in the identical outfit in every panel, consistent scale.

Style: posterized photograph with engraving texture — a real photo, run through a hard posterize/cutout filter and overprinted with fine etched hatching. Photographic realism first: looks like a real high-detail photograph of a real person in real costume, then posterized into hard-edged flat colour shapes — no soft gradients, no airbrush. Form and texture modelled by fine black hand-engraved crosshatching inside the shapes — NO outlines: edges defined only by colour and value contrast, never by a drawn black contour line, like a steel-plate etching printed over flat fills. Severely limited palette: deep ultramarine, saturated vermilion red, warm bone-white, ink black; metal rendered as posterized silver or gold with broken, high-contrast highlights. One hard sun, crisp shadows filled with flat black or dense hatching. Every flat area carries heavy uniform print grain with a faint misregistration halo at edges. Dense micro-texture everywhere — pitted stone, woven cloth, hammered metal, skin pores. High contrast, saturated, printed-poster finish. Not a cartoon, not a comic, not a drawing, not a flat vector illustration, no ink outlines, no panel borders or frames. No 3D render, no glossy digital painting, no bokeh, no soft focus.

## LOCATION BLOCK

Photographic realism first: looks like a real high-detail photograph of a real place, then posterized into hard-edged flat colour shapes — no soft gradients, no airbrush. Dense micro-texture everywhere — pitted stone, woven cloth, hammered metal, skin pores. High contrast, saturated, printed-poster finish. Not a cartoon, not a comic, not a drawing, not a flat vector illustration, no ink outlines, no panel borders or frames. No 3D render, no glossy digital painting, no bokeh, no haze gradients.
