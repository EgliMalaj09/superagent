# MARTESA EP1 — Office set: blocking & screen-direction bible (applies to EVERY office shot)

Top-down map (north = windows):

        [ FLOOR-TO-CEILING WINDOWS — night city, rain ]
                    ADRIANI (chair, faces south)
      LAMP  ┌───────────────────────────┐
     (green)│          DESK             │
            └───────────────────────────┘
                    ELIRA (chair, faces north)
        [ DARK BOOKSHELVES + DOOR — wood paneling ]

  CAMERA SIDE = WEST (lamp side). The camera NEVER crosses to the east side (180° rule).

Consequences — locked for all shots:
- ADRIANI is ALWAYS on FRAME LEFT, looking toward FRAME RIGHT.
- ELIRA is ALWAYS on FRAME RIGHT, looking toward FRAME LEFT.
- Behind ADRIANI = windows with blurred blue city lights + rain.
- Behind ELIRA  = dark wooden bookshelves / paneling (NEVER windows).
- The GREEN LAMP stands at the WEST end of the desk = Adriani's RIGHT hand, Elira's LEFT hand.
  It is always on FRAME LEFT. Warm green-gold key light falls on the frame-left side of both faces;
  frame-right side of faces is in cool blue shadow.
- Over-the-shoulder on Adriani: Elira's blurred shoulder/hair in FOREGROUND FRAME RIGHT.
- Over-the-shoulder on Elira: Adriani's blurred dark shoulder in FOREGROUND FRAME LEFT.
- Adriani's watch: LEFT wrist.

## Window corner (Scene 2 onward)
- The window wall continues past the desk into a corner (north-east). When Elira walks to the glass she goes to that corner.
- Camera still stays on the WEST (lamp) side. Rule still holds: ADRIANI = FRAME LEFT, ELIRA = FRAME RIGHT.
- At the window: Elira on FRAME RIGHT in three-quarter profile facing FRAME LEFT toward the glass; her faint reflection
  on the glass sits on FRAME LEFT over the blurred city lights; Adriani (soft, deep background) seated at the desk on
  FRAME LEFT with the green lamp glow.
