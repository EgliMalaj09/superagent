## Shared pipeline (used for every video)

1. **Voice is the master clock.** The voiceover (doctor take, plan video audio or a black-screen MP4) is never re-timed or re-recorded by us; every cut, caption and animation is placed on its word timings.
2. **Word timings:** faster-whisper `medium`, `compute_type="int8"`, `language="it"`, `word_timestamps=True`, an `initial_prompt` with brand words ("Dental Med Austria, Tirana, Lugano, laboratorio odontotecnico interno"). Decode audio with ffmpeg to 16 kHz float32 and pass the array (passing a file path failed with a PyAV `metadata_errors` error).
3. **Plan video first.** A black card per scene (rendered with the Remotion `C3Card` composition: "SKENA x", a coloured tag AI / REAL / ANIM, what to shoot, the Italian line, the seconds) placed on the voice, real footage dropped into its slots. The client and the user approve the structure before any AI or animation work.
4. **Base edit in ffmpeg** (`build.py` per video): every slot is `(start_frame, end_frame, source, source_start_s, filter, push_in, speed)`; 30 fps; ~0.33 s (10-frame) `xfade=fade` dissolves centred on each cut (5-frame handles each side); a 5 % slow push-in per shot; frame-exact length checked with `ffmpeg -map 0:v -c copy -f null - -stats`.
5. **Overlay in Remotion** (captions + animations) rendered transparent as a PNG sequence at `--scale=2` (4K), then composited over the base upscaled to 2160×3840 with ffmpeg.
6. **Export:** H.264 High, `-crf 16`, `-level 5.2`, bt709 tags, `-movflags +faststart`, AAC 256–320k, loudness `loudnorm=I=-14:TP=-1.0:LRA=11` (Instagram/TikTok).
7. **Check before sending:** contact sheets / stills at every cut and animation (`select='eq(n\,…)'`, `tile=`), plus a Whisper read-back of the final mix for intelligibility.

### Standard filters
- **Real camera footage (DSCF…, 2048×1080 rotated → 1080×2048):**
  `crop=1080:1920:0:(ih-1920)/2,curves=master='0/0 0.25/0.21 0.6/0.56 0.85/0.78 1/0.9',eq=saturation=1.1:contrast=1.04,colorbalance=rm=0.03:bm=-0.03:rh=0.02:bh=-0.02` (warm grade, matches the AI scenes)
- **AI / phone clips:** `scale=1080:1920:force_original_aspect_ratio=increase:flags=lanczos,crop=1080:1920` (+ `unsharp=5:5:0.5:3:3:0.2` for 720p phone footage)
- **Push-in:** `scale=w='trunc(1080*(1+0.05*n/L)/2)*2':h=-2:eval=frame,crop=1080:1920`
- **Low-res reference upscale (720×1280 Instagram):** `hqdn3d=1.5:1.2:3:3,scale=1080:1920:flags=lanczos+accurate_rnd+full_chroma_int,unsharp=5:5:0.75:3:3:0.2,eq=contrast=1.03:saturation=1.04`
- **4K final:** `scale=2160:3840:flags=lanczos,unsharp=5:5:0.35:5:5:0` + overlay PNGs.

### Caption style (approved, same in every video)
- Poppins ExtraBold (800), loaded locally from `remotion/public/fonts/Poppins-800.woff2`.
- 132 px at 1080 wide (rendered ×2 for 4K), `top: 1120` (just below centre), side margins 30 px, line-height 1.08.
- 1–3 words per chunk, each word pops in on its spoken time (spring damping 14 / stiffness 260 / mass 0.6, 3-frame fade).
- White words, **key words in yellow `#FFE14D` UPPERCASE**; soft dark text shadow.
- `fitSize()` shrinks a chunk if the widest word won't fit 1000 px, so long words (ODONTOTECNICO) stay on one line; max 2 lines.
- Chunk stays until the next one, max +0.9 s after its last word; captions stop where the logo end card starts.

### Animation style (approved)
- Dark navy pills `rgba(12,22,38,0.84)` with gold `#E2C17A` border, Poppins 800, gold/yellow round icon; yellow variant for the "answer" item.
- Gold pin that drops with a spring and a ground ripple, label pill under it (e.g. "TIRANA · ALBANIA").
- Spring pops in, 8-frame ease-out exits, synced to the exact word.
- Placement: top third or under the captions (y ≥ 1500 when captions can be 2 lines); **never over a face**.
- One animation idea per shot; emotional shots get captions only.

### AI generation
- Seedance/Higgsfield prompts written by us, generated by the user (Videos 2–3); later generated directly through the user's `~/Desktop/api-keys/day7` project:
  - keyframe: `python3 gen.py prompt.txt kf.png --aspect 9:16 --ref <reference frames>` (Gemini 3 Pro Image on Vertex AI, ADC login via `~/google-cloud-sdk/bin/gcloud`)
  - video: `python3 omni.py prompt.txt clip.mp4 --image kf.png --aspect 9:16 --duration 4s|6s --resolution 1080p` (Gemini Omni Flash)
- References are real frames pulled from the client footage (`ffmpeg -ss … -frames:v 1`), so wardrobe, props and the clinic match.

## Video 1 — "A chi stai affidando il tuo sorriso?" (Creative 01)

**What:** doctor hook on camera, then b-roll of the clinic (reception, team, lab, 1,000 m², 19 chairs), Tirana → Firenze route map, Seedance consultation clip, logo.
**Format/length:** 9:16, 52.3 s (1569–1570 frames @30 fps). Final masters: `finished-1-arzi-FINAL-captions-v3.mp4` (1440×2560) → `-v4-4K.mp4` → `-v5-4K.mp4` (2160×3840).

**Pipeline specifics**
- Hook (0–11.97 s, 359 frames) kept as burned-in footage (`build/rec49/hook_hl.mp4`); body from a clean master (`rec49/cleanswap.mp4`) + Remotion `CaptionsOnlyBig` PNGs at 4K.

**Style:** warm grade; captions as above; hook untouched except yellow keywords.

**Changed / rejected:**

- Reception handshake shot replaced (it showed the man who appears again at the end) → tight crop of the receptionist alone from DSCF9519 (8.8–10.5 s, `crop=440:782:370:680`), so nobody appears twice.

### Video 1 city variants (Firenze → Lugano → Torino)
- **Firenze:** the original master — route map lands on Firenze at 43.52 s on the word "Firenze"; caption "a FIRENZE".
- **Lugano:** WhatsApp voice take ("Dopo il rientro, i nostri riferimenti a Lugano…"), cut up to "Lugano", studio "…possono seguirti…" kept. `RouteMap` made configurable (`dest="lugano"`, label above the pin, Switzerland lit). Caption swap via `PopCaptions swap={{Firenze:"Lugano"}}`.
- **Torino:** `finished-1-torino-4K-v2/v3/v4.mp4`. Voice note `.ogg` cleaned (`highpass=f=80,afftdn=nr=8:nf=-50:tn=1,acompressor=threshold=-24dB:ratio=2…`), tone-matched to the studio voice with a 1/3-octave spectral EQ (±8 dB), dropped in **as one continuous piece** so "Torino" lands at 43.4 s on the pin.
- **Removing the old word from the mix:** subtracting the raw voice failed ("Firenze" still audible); rebuilding from the music stem failed (stem didn't match). What worked: per-channel Wiener-fitted voice chain (`build/lugano/vo3.py`): `residual = mix − conv(voice, H)`, `new = residual + conv(new_voice, H)` only in 40.0–44.22 s with 40 ms crossfades; Whisper on the residual heard no words.
- **"in Tirana" → caption "a Tirana"** (voice unchanged).
- **Music from second 0:** regenerated the same score with 2 extra bars before 3 s on the same grid (`build/torino/music_ext.py`), added at the measured under-voice gain (0.272, −11.3 dB) — later replaced entirely.
- **Rhythmic score (v4):** voice separated from the old music (cancellation −21.8 → −55 dB in gaps), new original 120 BPM groove (`build/torino/groove.py`: four-on-the-floor kick, claps on 2&4, 16th hats, driving 8th bass, pluck arpeggio, kick-pump sidechain, light under the hook, full beat at 12 s, lift at 25 s, one-beat break into the map, final hit 51 s), ducked 6 dB under the voice, music ~13 dB below voice, −14 LUFS.

**Rejected in variants:** cutting the new take into pieces to match the old pacing — it clipped "riferimenti" and left a gap ("stucking"). Fixed by keeping the take continuous.

## Reusable rules and best practices

**Structure & approval**
1. Build a plan video with black placeholder cards on the real voice before generating anything; get sign-off, then fill.
2. Map every scene to exact word timings (faster-whisper medium, int8, word timestamps); cut on word boundaries.
3. Prefer the client's real footage; use AI only where nothing real fits. Never reuse a sequence that already appeared in another video of the same campaign.
4. Keep each shot 1.5–6 s; ~0.33 s dissolves centred on cuts; 5 % push-in per shot. Speed changes and motion-blur "timelapse" effects look bad — keep real-time speed and clean cuts.

**Voice & audio**
5. The voiceover is the master: never re-time or alter it; strip only handling noise before the first word.
6. Keep voice and music stems for every master — they make later voice/city swaps possible.
7. To replace a word inside a finished mix: fit the mix's voice chain per channel (Wiener FIR), subtract the old voice, add the new one through the same chain, only inside the edited window, 40 ms crossfades. Verify with Whisper on the residual.
8. New takes from voice notes: highpass 80 Hz, light `afftdn` (nr 8), gentle compression, 1/3-octave EQ match to the studio voice, level match per sentence — and keep the sentence **continuous** (don't splice words to match old pacing).
9. Music: original synthesized score, D major, 120 BPM groove for energy (96 BPM piano felt too slow); duck under the voice; final level ≈4–6 dB under the voice; −14 LUFS, TP −1 dB. No SFX on doctor-led ads.

**Captions & animation**
10. Poppins 800, 132 px @1080 (×2 for 4K), top 1120, word pops, yellow `#FFE14D` caps keywords, max 2 lines, auto-shrink long words.
11. Animations: navy/gold pills, gold pins, spring pops, timed to the exact word; one idea per shot; nothing over faces; keep labels on one line (nowrap, widen the pill).
12. Doctor-on-camera parts in Videos 2–3 get animations (Video 1's hook stays as shot, only yellow keywords).
13. End card = clinic logo only, centred, fading in; no CTA text/button.

**AI generation**
14. Doctor's face is never AI — shoot from behind. Patients: lips closed, no teeth, no before/after.
15. Use real reference frames for people, props and locations; give a face reference to stop drift; generate 4–6 s and use the best 2–3 s.
16. One prop per shot; write "bare hands"; avoid words that get printed literally (e.g. "A4").
17. When a character must stay anonymous, make an object the identity device (the blue suitcase + watch) and keep framing at hand/waist height until the real-footage reveal.

**Quality control**
18. 4K export: lanczos upscale + light unsharp, overlay rendered natively at 4K; H.264 CRF 16, bt709 tags, faststart.
19. Check every cut and animation on stills, check frame counts, and run Whisper on the final mix before delivering.
20. Watch disk space before 4K renders (Remotion copies `public/` per render — stash unused assets; keep ≥5 GB free).
